# 02. Competition rules and logistics

Source: `RULES AI Control Layer.pdf` (terms and conditions, an annex to the general HackYeah T&C). Organiser and prize sponsor (legal entity): Proidea Sp. z o.o., Kraków.

## Timeline
- Work may start no earlier than **"11:00 PM on October 3rd"** (as written in the PDF; probably a typo for 11:00 AM, since the event is already running on 3 Oct). Work must be **submitted no later than 23:00 on 4 October 2026**.
- Any change made after the deadline is not allowed and will not be considered.
- The jury list will be posted on the HackYeah Discord no later than 4 October.

## Team
- Individuals or teams of **up to 6 people**.
- People related to jury members or to employees of the prize sponsor are excluded.

## What to submit (HackTribe platform, English or Polish; the challenge page requires **English**)
- a) Project title
- b) Team name
- c) List of team members (1-6)
- d) Project description
- e) **PDF presentation, max 10 slides**. It may include screenshots, code repository link, demo links, graphics, and other materials the task suggests (architecture diagram, sample config, dashboard screenshots, test results).

## Judging process
- **Phase 1:** submitted projects on HackTribe are evaluated by a commission of at least 3 mentors.
- **Phase 2:** finalists chosen in phase 1 **pitch live** to a jury. The phase 1 commission and the jury may overlap.
- A project needs **at least 50% of the points in phase 1** to be eligible for a prize. Decisions are final.
- The jury picks a chairman. On a tie, the jury vote decides.

## Prizes (incl. tax)
1st: 6,000 PLN. 2nd: 5,000 PLN. 3rd: 4,000 PLN. Paid within 90 days of results. Copyright stays with the authors.
